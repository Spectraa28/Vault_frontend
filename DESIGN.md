# The Vault

A playable nocturnal diorama, not a marketing page. The world owns the screen. Interface pieces look like objects from the bank: an engraved ID badge, a teller receipt, stamped paper, brass medallions.

Palette: bottle green #102d26, near-black #081510, aged brass #c6a365, ivory #eee5cf, warm light #ffce87, cool night #7a9fae. Didone-style Georgia for titles, humanist Trebuchet for interface, Courier for receipts; system fonts keep the offline build self-contained.

Opening screen: bank on the right, restrained title on the left, one Walk in action. No cards or dashboard grids. Cinematic visual hierarchy with a thin frame and small chapter labels. Desktop conversations reserve the right side for the teller tape. Mobile uses a compact scrollable paper roll above the dialogue.

Interaction: visible keyboard focus, 44px controls, textual stamp labels, E to talk, WASD/arrows movement, Q/R camera rotation, Escape to leave, reduced-motion camera cuts. Replay status is always visible. A recorded session never claims that current text or role produced its saved decisions.

Geometry first: low-poly coat-and-hat figures over capsule bodies; procedural bank, marble, metal wheel, rain and dust. Replace with licensed, compressed GLBs only after assets exist. Preserve idle/walk/jog/mood animation hooks. No remote assets required to boot.
